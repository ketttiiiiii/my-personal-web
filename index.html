My academic pursuit is rooted in understanding how service ecosystems drive modern economies. At Tzu Chi University, the curriculum transcends traditional business management by embedding a profound sense of social responsibility into industrial practices.

The core of my studies focuses on the optimization of service delivery networks, cross-cultural management strategies, and the integration of sustainable practices within large-scale operations. I am particularly fascinated by how organizational behavior and strategic planning can transform a standard service into an exceptional, value-driven experience.

Through rigorous coursework in quality management, service design, and international business strategy, I have developed a robust framework for analyzing and improving complex operational structures.
